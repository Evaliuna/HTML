## 🤔 Why this exists

Most HTML tutorials show you a tag, then make you open an editor to see what it does.
This one doesn't. **Every tag is rendered right on the page**, next to a short explanation of what it's for and when to use it.

No frameworks. No build step. No install. Just one `index.html` file.

## 👀 What's inside

| # | Section | You'll learn |
|---|---------|--------------|
| 1 | **Structure & Semantics** | `header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, `aside`, `footer` |
| 2 | **Text & Content** | headings, `strong`, `em`, `mark`, `blockquote`, `pre`, `code` |
| 3 | **Lists** | `ul`, `ol`, `dl` and how they differ |
| 4 | **Images & Media** | `figure`, `img`, `video`, `audio` |
| 5 | **Forms & Input** | text, email, color, checkbox, radio, select, textarea |
| 6 | **Links & Tables** | `a`, `table`, `thead`, `tbody`, `th`, `td` |
| 7 | **Advanced Elements** | `iframe`, `canvas`, `svg`, `datalist`, `dialog`, `ruby` |
| 8 | **The Invisible Work** | `meta`, `link`, `noscript` and what lives in `<head>` |
| 9 | **Rare Tags** | `address`, `time`, `data`, `kbd`, `samp`, `var`, `dfn`, `bdo` |
| 10 | **Templates** | `template` and how it powers modern frameworks |
| 11 | **History Museum** | deprecated tags (`marquee`, `center`, `font`) and why to avoid them |
| 12 | **Global Attributes** | `id`, `class`, `style`, `tabindex`, `contenteditable`, and more |

## 🧭 Who is this for?

- 🌱 **Absolute beginners** who want to see every tag in action
- 🎓 **Students** revising for exams or interviews
- 🔁 **Developers** who need a quick "what was that tag again?" reference
- 🧑‍🏫 **Teachers** who want a single page to project in class
